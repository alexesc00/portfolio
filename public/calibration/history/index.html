Question history

Each question's whole history is on one screen, from its first review to how candidates do in production, so the team can see whether each edit worked. The sample shows Q5 four weeks in, after 6 of 31 candidates ran out of time.

Notes

  1. 1

    Nothing is reused. The brief says nothing records why a question changed or whether the change worked, and this page is that record for Q5.

  2. 2

    False comparisons. Each row says who sat it, since no two batches of testers were alike.

  3. 3

    Testers ≠ candidates. No tester ran out of time on version 3, but 6 of 31 candidates did. Their 1.3 pace sets how future tester times get read.

  4. 4

    Attribution. Q5 builds on Q4, so some of its blanks come from Q4, a cause that shows only across questions.

  5. 5

    Nobody edits a live test, so every candidate sits the same one. Q5 isn't broken, and dropping it would leave verification unscored.
