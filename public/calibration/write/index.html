Write questions

The author marks what to test in the client's job description, then writes each question and its rubric on this screen instead of in a doc. The sample shows them linking Q6, the last of six, to the requirements it tests.

Notes

  1. 1

    Written here, not in a doc. Almost nobody uses the platform today, so writing here has to be as quick as the doc.

  2. 2

    A new step 0. A fair question can still test something the client didn't ask for, so the author marks what to test in the client's own words.

  3. 3

    Each question links to a marked requirement, and the links become the owner's coverage check at promotion.

  4. 4

    Written to work without its author, since someone else may grade in production.

  5. 5

    Attribution. Results get read against this intent, and tagging the tool lets trouble on Q2 and Q4, both spreadsheets, point to the platform.

  6. 6

    Nothing is reused. Sending replaces the brief's paste into the platform, so every comment, reply and edit stays with its question.
